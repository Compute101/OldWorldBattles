Old World Battles

Battle reports from the tabletops of Warhammer: The Old World

This site is a record of games played of Warhammer: The Old World. Each battle report covers the armies involved, the scenario, and how the fighting unfolded turn by turn, along with the final result. It's mostly here so we can look back on memorable battles, track how our armies perform over time, and share reports with the rest of the group.
