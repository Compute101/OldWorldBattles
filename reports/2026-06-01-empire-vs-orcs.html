The Siege of Grimmenhagen

Date played: 1 June 2026 · Points: 2000 · Scenario: Meeting Engagement

Empire of Man

General Voss's Grimmenhagen Garrison

VS

Orc & Goblin Tribes

Gorbash Ironjaw's Waaagh!

The Armies

The Empire fielded a solid gunline anchored by two cannons and a unit of Handgunners, with a block of Greatswords and a detachment of Knights held in reserve to counter-charge. Gorbash's Waaagh! brought a huge mob of Orc Boyz, a unit of Boar Boyz for speed, and a Rock Lobber to answer the Empire's guns.

The Battle

The greenskins advanced eagerly under cover of their Rock Lobber, which found the range on the Empire cannon line by turn two, silencing one gun outright. The Boar Boyz swung wide on the flank, threatening the Empire's war machines, but were caught in the open by a well-timed cannonball and broke before they reached combat.

In the centre, the Orc Boyz mob crashed into the Greatswords. The fighting was brutal and prolonged, with the Empire line bowing but not breaking, held together by General Voss fighting in the front rank. On the following turn, the Empire Knights arrived on the flank and slammed into the mob's exposed side, and the greenskins' resolve finally failed.

The Result

Empire of Man victory. The garrison held Grimmenhagen, though at heavy cost — the Greatswords were reduced to a handful of survivors, and one cannon was lost to the Rock Lobber.

Notes

The flank charge from the Knights was the turning point; committing them to reserve rather than the initial battle line paid off. Next game: more screening for the war machines against warmachine-hunting units like Boar Boyz.
