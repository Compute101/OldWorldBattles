Tomb of the Sundered King

Date played: 15 June 2026 · Points: 2000 · Scenario: Dawn Attack

Dwarfen Mountain Holds

Thorgrim Ironbeard's Relic Expedition

VS

Tomb Kings of Khemri

The Sundered King's Legion

The Armies

Thorgrim's expedition was a slow, hard-hitting force: two units of Dwarf Warriors, a block of Longbeards, an Organ Gun, and a Gyrocopter for scouting. The Tomb Kings answered with a large Skeleton Warrior regiment, Tomb Guard as the Sundered King's honour guard, a Screaming Skull Catapult, and a unit of Carrion for harassment.

The Battle

The Dwarfs advanced in a deliberate line, using the Gyrocopter to screen the Organ Gun from the Carrion's charge threat. The Screaming Skull Catapult misfired on turn one and did not fire again for the rest of the game, a huge break for the Dwarf gunline.

The Skeleton Warriors reached the Dwarf line first and were ground down by disciplined rank-and-file fighting, the Dwarfs' superior weapon skill telling over several rounds of combat. The Tomb Guard, with the Sundered King himself in the ranks, fared better against the Longbeards, and the combat there see-sawed for three full turns before the Dwarfs' stubborn resolve finally won out.

The Result

Dwarfen Mountain Holds victory. The Sundered King's legion was broken and scattered back into the sand, and Thorgrim's expedition claimed the tomb's relics.

Notes

The Screaming Skull Catapult misfire was a big swing in the Dwarfs' favour — without it, the Organ Gun likely gets picked off early and the game looks very different. Good showing for the Gyrocopter as a screening unit.
